A smaller example uses separate synthetic data to illustrate the method.
```stata
clear all
set more off
set type double
input town time adoption level noise
1 2 3 10 0
1 3 3 10 .2
1 4 3 10 -.1
2 2 3 14 .1
2 3 3 14 -.2
2 4 3 14 .3
3 2 0 8 0
3 3 0 8 .1
3 4 0 8 -.1
4 2 5 12 .1
4 3 5 12 0
4 4 5 12 .2
5 2 4 9 0
5 3 4 9 .5
5 4 4 9 .8
6 2 0 15 .1
6 3 0 15 .3
6 4 0 15 -.2
end
gen double y=level+.5*time+2*(adoption==3 & time>=3)+noise
drop if town==6 & time==3
keep if adoption==3 | adoption==0 | adoption>4
bysort town: gen complete=(_N==3)
keep if complete
gen byte did=(adoption==3 & time>=3)
* regress includes the intercept and omits one level of each fixed effect.
quietly regress y did i.town i.time
scalar effect=_b[did]
scalar rows=e(N)
quietly count if adoption==3
scalar towns=r(N)/3
display "ANSWER: town_effect estimate=" %24.16g effect " se=NA n_obs=" %12.0f rows
display "ANSWER: treated_towns estimate=" %24.16g towns " se=NA n_obs=" %12.0f rows
exit, clear
```
