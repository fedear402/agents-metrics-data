Example on separate small data. This simpler example illustrates a related calculation.
```stata
clear
input double x
-0.5
0.5
end
* A simpler known-parameter hurdle model on two covariate rows.
generate double p = invlogit(-0.2+0.4*x)
generate double lam = exp(0.1+0.3*x)
generate double q = 1-exp(-lam)
generate double conditional = lam/q
generate double mu = p*conditional
generate double derivative = p*(1-p)*0.4*conditional+p*0.3*lam*(q-lam*exp(-lam))/q^2
generate double tail = p*poissontail(lam,3)/q
quietly summarize mu
display "mean_y " %15.10f r(mean)
quietly summarize derivative
display "ame_x " %15.10f r(mean)
quietly summarize tail
display "prob_at_least_three " %15.10f r(mean)
```

Task:

Conditional on y>0, fit a zero-truncated Poisson with log lambda=b0+b_x*x+b_d*d, using only positive observations. Its likelihood is the Poisson mass divided by 1-exp(-lambda). The unconditional mean is Pr(y>0)*lambda/(1-exp(-lambda)). Report mean_y, the equally weighted average unconditional fitted mean over all rows. Report ame_x, the average derivative of this unconditional mean with respect to continuous x, accounting for x in both fitted equations. Report effect_d, the average difference in this unconditional mean when d changes from zero to one in both equations. Keep x at its observed value. Also report prob_at_least_three, the equally weighted average fitted unconditional probability Pr(y>=3|x,d) over all supplied rows. This probability includes the hurdle probability and the zero-truncation adjustment. For all results, n_obs is the full dataset row count, including zeros.
