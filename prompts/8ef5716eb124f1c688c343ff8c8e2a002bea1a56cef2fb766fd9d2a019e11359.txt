Related example: Anderson-Rubin inversion with two encouragement instruments
Separate example context: Ten separate teaching farms provide two encouragement assignments, fertilizer dose, and harvest. Assume both assignments satisfy the instrument conditions. Include an intercept and invert the homoskedastic joint F test.
Explanation of this example: Let q count excluded instruments and df=n-p count residual degrees of freedom in the unrestricted regression. F(beta)=((SSR_R(beta)-SSR_U(beta))/q)/(SSR_U(beta)/df). Acceptance is SSR_R-(1+q*critical/df)*SSR_U<=0. Expand this into A*beta^2+B*beta+C<=0. For two distinct real boundaries, A>0 accepts the interval and A<0 accepts the outer rays. Derive the sign and membership from the example data. Other data can require separate degenerate-set handling.

st_numscalar("low",bounds[1]); st_numscalar("high",bounds[2])

// Acceptance is between boundaries for A>0 and on both outer rays for A<0.
