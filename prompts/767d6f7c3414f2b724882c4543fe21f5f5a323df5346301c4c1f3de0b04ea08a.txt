Example on separate small data. This simpler example illustrates a related calculation.
```r
# A simpler known-parameter hurdle model on two covariate rows.
x <- c(-0.5,0.5); p <- plogis(-0.2+0.4*x); lam <- exp(0.1+0.3*x)
q <- -expm1(-lam); conditional <- lam/q
mu <- p*conditional
derivative <- p*(1-p)*0.4*conditional+p*0.3*lam*(q-lam*exp(-lam))/q^2
cat('mean_y', sprintf('%.10f', mean(mu)), '\n')
cat('ame_x', sprintf('%.10f', mean(derivative)), '\n')
cat('prob_at_least_three', sprintf('%.10f', mean(p*ppois(2,lam,lower.tail=FALSE)/q)), '\n')
```

The unconditional mean is Pr(y>0)*lambda/(1-exp(-lambda)). Report mean_y, the equally weighted average unconditional fitted mean over all rows. Report ame_x, the average derivative of this unconditional mean with respect to continuous x, accounting for x in both fitted equations. Report effect_d, the average difference in this unconditional mean when d changes from zero to one in both equations. Keep x at its observed value. This probability includes the hurdle probability and the zero-truncation adjustment. For all results, n_obs is the full dataset row count, including zeros.
